# Your First Week on VaiLyf: an onboarding film

Style: Isometric Infographic (`iso-infographic`). 1080×1920 (Instagram Reels / WhatsApp status), 30 s. English narration (warm female), English subtitles as map-label cards. For new employees.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A day route.** One employee walks a route across a flat isometric office board: desk, canteen, stairs, bed, phone. Clear, but horizontal routes fight a vertical frame, and a walking route is the style's demo grammar (a supply chain followed along a path).
2. **Isotype list.** Each feature is an Isotype number (8 drops, 20 points, 5 streak days…) on an empty board. Precise, but a list with no place and no person; for onboarding it never says *where* things are.
3. **One building, floor by floor (chosen).** The portal is a tower; each floor is one thing you do on it. The camera starts on the roof title and tilts down floor by floor; each floor's front skin is cut away to show the feature inside, with a "you are here" ring on the new employee. At the bottom we pull back to the whole tower: the finished infographic, every label on screen. A vertical frame is made for a tower, and the floors map one-to-one onto the portal's menu (Home → Daily Log → Challenges → Programs → Body Tracker), so a new user learns the order they'll meet.

**Opening:** the isometric title YOUR FIRST WEEK ON VAILYF extruded on the tower's roof, with a single green figure stepping onto it.
**Ending:** pull back to the whole tower, every floor labelled; it becomes the legend card with VaiLyf Wellness and the website.
**Score shape:** a light kalimba-and-marimba motif that adds one instrument per floor (accumulation), a short drop-out before the pull-back, then all of it together on the reveal.

## 2. Logline and arc

A new employee's first week on VaiLyf, as a tower you explore floor by floor: log your day, earn points, join challenges, follow your coach, see your progress.

- **Setup:** "New to VaiLyf?" A tower, a new user on the roof.
- **Turn:** each floor opens to one habit loop; points and streak build up as she goes down.
- **Ending:** the whole tower at once: the portal is one connected system, and it starts with today's log.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Isometric cutaway building posters and tower infographics** (as a genre). Learn: one floor = one function, stacked; cut faces with hatched rims; labels pinned outside the building. Don't take: any specific poster's building or icons.
- **Short app-onboarding animations** (as a genre). Learn: one action per screen, a 3–4 s rhythm, the user's own figure as the guide. Don't take: any app's UI, characters or music.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

- Jost (OFL): floor titles 600 caps, tracking 0.12 em; numbers 400; all text with a 7 px ground-colour halo.
- Subtitle as a map label: pale card, ink border, tiny iso cube chip, Jost ~40 px, bottom-centre at y ≈ 1500–1600 (inside Instagram's safe area), border first then text wipes in.
- Floor labels on pins outside the tower, alternating left and right so they never cover the cutaway.
