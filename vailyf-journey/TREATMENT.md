# Meera's Journal: a VaiLyf Wellness film

Style: Watercolor Brush (`watercolor`). 1920×1080, 60 fps, ~48 s. Hindi narration (hi-IN-SwaraNeural, warm female), Hindi subtitles with English beneath.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A day transect.** One long lateral dolly across a continuous painted world from dawn to night (wake, commute, desk, lunch, walk, sleep), first in grey, then repainted in colour. Clear, but it's the style's signature move (a long walk), and "a day" can't show change over weeks.
2. **Specimen plates.** Each habit (water, food, steps, sleep, mood) is a labelled botanical-style plate, page after page. Elegant, but a list with no turn: no person, no story.
3. **A journal kept over four weeks (chosen).** Meera's own wellness journal. One sheet opens on a hand-written question; habits are painted onto it as specimens with notes; a bad day greys the page; her team's footprints join hers; then the whole palette turns from grey-ochre to green as her score rises and the wilted desk plant blooms. It ends on the same sheet with a new question. This gives one subject, one goal and one turn, the score can be a colour, and a locked page painted over time is a move only this medium can do.

**Opening image:** a hand-written question on blank paper, "मैं इतनी थकी क्यों हूँ?" ("Why am I so tired?").
**Ending:** the same question struck through by a brush stroke, a new line written beneath, "आज क्या नया करूँ?" ("What new thing shall I try today?"), then the page mists to the end card.
**Score shape:** starts sparse and tired (solo felt piano on a minor 7th), builds with kalimba and fingerpicked guitar as habits stack, cuts to silence at the dip, then the full ensemble with flute at the bloom, resolving gently on the end card.

## 2. Logline and arc

A tired office worker keeps a small daily wellness log with VaiLyf, and four weeks of tiny habits repaint her journal from grey to green.

- **Setup:** Meera is tired: short sleep, three coffees, a wilted desk plant.
- **Turn:** she starts a daily log; habits paint in one by one. One grey day she wants to quit, and her team's footprints walk in beside hers.
- **Ending:** the score rises, the page turns green, the plant blooms; her question changes from "why am I tired?" to "what new thing shall I try?".

## 3. Benchmark

- **Botanical field journals and naturalist sketchbooks** (as a genre). Learn: specimen + hand note + pen leader line; generous margins; one hero per view. Don't take: any particular plate or layout.
- **Gentle public-health and charity explainers** (as a genre). Learn: one person standing for many, numbers written small and human, warmth over slogans. Don't take: any campaign's characters, logos or music.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

- Subtitles on the paper, centred bottom, no box: Hindi in Tiro Devanagari Hindi (OFL) at ~40 px, English beneath in Cormorant Garamond italic at ~26 px; ink at 78 % over a paper-coloured halo.
- Hand notes: Kalam (OFL), a Devanagari and Latin handwriting face, written left to right with pen leader lines. Measured values (6,000 · 7 h · 42 → 78) in IBM Plex Mono with wide tracking.
- Titles: "मीरा की डायरी" ("Meera's Journal") as a quiet hand title top-left of the page.
- Numbers spelled out in the voice text, written as digits on screen. "VaiLyf" is spoken "वाइलाइफ़" and written VaiLyf.
