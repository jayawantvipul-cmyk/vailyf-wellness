# VaiLyf Whiteboard Tips: a 3-reel series (Hydration · Sleep · Movement)

Style: Whiteboard Explainer (`whiteboard`). Three vertical reels, 1080×1920, ~20 s each. Hindi narration (hi-IN-SwaraNeural, warm female), Hindi on the board, English subtitles. For employees and anyone at a desk job. End card: **"BOOK" कमेंट करें** (comment BOOK to book a wellness session) + VaiLyf Wellness + vailyfai.com + 1/3 · 2/3 · 3/3.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Three separate boards.** Each reel is its own clean board: question, 3 tips, end card. Simple, but the series has no thread: nothing says "this is part 2".
2. **A checklist.** One list on the board, ticked item by item. Fast, but a list of words is not a whiteboard explainer: the drawing does none of the explaining.
3. **One board, three regions (chosen).** The series is drawn on one long board. Reel 1 fills the left region (water), reel 2 the middle (sleep), reel 3 the right (movement). Each reel opens on a question with a drawing, writes three numbered tips as small drawings with a Hindi label, then the camera pulls back to show the board *so far*: after reel 1 one region is full and two are blank; after reel 3 the whole board is one picture: a daily routine. The pull-back is the series' "part x of 3" made visible, and a viewer who finds reel 3 first sees the other two waiting on the board.

**Opening of each reel:** a big question drawn as a picture (a drained battery, an alarm clock, a chair) with a short Hindi question.
**Ending of each reel:** the pull-back across the board, the tip numbers circled, then the end card written in the free space below: BOOK कमेंट करें.
**Score shape:** a light plucked figure (kalimba and pizzicato) that adds one instrument per tip; a full stop into silence just before the end card; the same motif in all three reels, a step higher each time (series identity).

## 2. Logline and arc (per reel)

Three tiny habits for one part of your day, drawn live on one board.

- **Setup:** a relatable question (tired by afternoon? tired as soon as you wake? sitting all day?).
- **Turn:** three habits, each one stroke-drawn as it's spoken.
- **Ending:** the pull-back shows the habits as part of one routine; a call to action to book a session.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Hand-drawn whiteboard explainers** (as a genre): one sentence = one drawing; the camera travels across one board instead of cutting. Don't take: any drawings, hands or music.
- **Short health-tip reels** (as a genre): a 2-second hook, numbered tips, one call to action. Don't take: any creator's graphics or catchphrases.

## 5. Colour logic (three markers, held across the series)

## 7. Cue map (each reel)

96 bpm (beat 0.625 s), the motif transposed up a step per reel (D → E → F#).

| Time | Section | Music | Hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3.2 | Hook | kalimba motif alone | the question finishes on the spoken word |
| 3.2–6.8 | Tip ① | + soft pizzicato bass | number circle on a downbeat |
| 6.8–10.4 | Tip ② | + shaker | number circle on a downbeat |
| 10.4–14.0 | Tip ③ | + glockenspiel | number circle on a downbeat |
| 14.0–14.6 | Stop | silence (room tone only) | marker cap click |
| 14.6–20 | Pull-back + end card | all, resolving | "BOOK" circled on the beat |

## 8. Sound design

Every stroke sounds (felt-on-gloss hiss + body, nib tick, occasional squeak), panned by its screen x; marker cap off/on; a quiet room tone with a wall-clock tick that owns the silence. Voice on top; music ducked under speech; foley ~11 dB under voice.

## 9. Type and subtitles

Board text: Hindi in a handwriting face (Kalam), written by a pen-led left-to-right reveal (no single-line Devanagari font exists); Latin and digits ("BOOK", "7–9", "10") in the style's single-line hand, stroke by stroke. Subtitles: off-white rounded label, Hindi line in Tiro Devanagari Hindi with the English line in a handwriting face beneath, short green marker dash, inside Instagram's safe area (bottom of label at y ≈ 1600).
