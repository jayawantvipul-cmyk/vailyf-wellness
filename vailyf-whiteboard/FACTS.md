# Facts used in the reels

| Claim | Where | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Adults should get 7–9 hours of sleep | Reel 2, tip 3 | National Sleep Foundation, "How Much Sleep Do You Need?" (recommended range for adults 18–64: 7–9 hours), https://www.thensf.org/how-many-hours-of-sleep-do-you-really-need/ |

All other tips (a glass of water on waking, a bottle at the desk, water with meals, a regular sleep schedule,
screens off before bed, standing up hourly, a short walk after lunch, taking the stairs) are general habits
presented without figures. They are wellness tips, not medical advice.
