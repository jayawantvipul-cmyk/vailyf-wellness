# Keep Going (गिरना हार नहीं है): a motivational reel

Style: One-line Drawing (`one-line`). 1080×1920, ~30 s, 24 fps. Hindi narration (hi-IN-SwaraNeural, warm female), English subtitles. VaiLyf Wellness.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A walking figure.** One line draws a person who walks, trips and stands up. Literal, but a figure redrawn pose by pose needs many separate drawings joined by stray connectors (the style's main pitfall), and the fall becomes a cartoon pratfall.
2. **A word.** The line writes "STOP", which unravels into "GO". Neat, but it's type, not drawing, and it needs reading in English.
3. **The line is a life (chosen).** The line itself is the climber: it runs uphill, falls off a cliff and **stops**. The ink pools into a teardrop blot and the music cuts to silence. Then it starts again, thin and trembling, gathers pressure step by step, climbs higher than before, and touches a gold sun at the peak (the one accent). A pull-back reveals that the whole line was one mountain range, the fall a valley in it. The medium is the message: the line never lifts, and the only failure the film shows is the moment it stops.

**Opening:** mid-line at speed, already running uphill. **Ending:** the pull-back to the whole range with the sun, the key line written below, then the line runs off the edge of the paper and keeps going. **Score:** one solo cello line that climbs with the drawing, falls with it, goes silent at the stop, returns as single tentative notes, and reaches its highest note on the sun.

## 2. Logline and arc

A line falls, stops, and keeps going: falling isn't failing, stopping is.

- Setup: the climb and the fall ("हर कोई गिरता है").
- Turn: the stop, then the first trembling stroke after it.
- Ending: higher than before; the whole journey seen as one range.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Continuous-line animation** (as a genre): one gesture, transformations instead of cuts. Learn: confidence and speed of the line; don't take any character or drawing.
- **Motivational reels** (as a genre): hook in 2 s, struggle, turn at the midpoint, message at the peak, share CTA. Don't take any creator's graphics, music or catchphrases.

## 6. Cue map

Free tempo (the line leads); solo cello. Rising phrase 0–4.5 (D minor → F); a falling run on the drop (4.5–6.8); **silence 7.0–9.2** (pen sound only, then nothing); single held notes at each trembling step 10–15; the phrase returns and climbs 15–20; the highest note (A5-ish, harmonic) on the sun at ~21; a warm resolve under v5 and the pull-back; last note fades as the line runs off the paper.

## 7. Sound design

The pen on paper is the main sound (band-passed noise from pen speed, fibre ticks, panned by nib position); a wooden tap at the first touch after the stop; a faint wet swell at the blot; literal silence at the stop (music bus and room tone at zero). Voice on top; music ducks ~5 dB, pen ~4 dB under voice; −14 LUFS.

## 8. Type

Subtitles written on in a handwriting face, no box, near the bottom (Hindi Kalam line, English Caveat-like line beneath), inside Instagram's safe area. The key line is written on the paper below the range at the end; VaiLyf Wellness in a monoline script.
